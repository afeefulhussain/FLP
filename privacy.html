Privacy Policy

Future Leaders Parliament (FLP) is dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of transparency, confidentiality, and data integrity for all members, delegates, and visitors.

Effective & Last Updated: October 2026

01 Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Future Leaders Parliament (FLP) collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information gathered from users ("Delegates", "Members", "Applicants", or "Visitors") of our official portal (https://afeefulhussain.github.io/FLP/).

By accessing our platform, submitting membership applications, or registering for FLP youth summits and webinars, you acknowledge and agree to the data practices described in this policy.

02 Information We Collect

We collect personal identification information from users in various ways, including when users visit our site, fill out the membership registration form, contact the Secretariat, or register for parliamentary events:

  • Personal Identity Data: Full Name, CNIC / B-Form / Passport (if required for credentials), Date of Birth, Gender.
  • Contact Details: Official Email Address, WhatsApp / Mobile Phone Number, Postal City/Province.
  • Academic & Professional Information: Educational Institution, Degree Program, Occupation, and Prior Leadership Experience.
  • Application Statements: Personal motivation essays, statement of purpose, department preferences, and policy interests.
  • Technical Browsing Data: Anonymized IP address, browser type, device information, and interaction timestamps.

03 How We Use Collected Information

Future Leaders Parliament uses the collected data exclusively for legitimate non-profit youth governance, leadership development, and operational purposes:

  • To evaluate and process membership applications and allocate delegates to their preferred parliamentary departments.
  • To organize, manage, and coordinate national webinars, masterclasses, and annual summits (e.g., Women's Empowerment Summit & Nissa Awards, Law Masterclass).
  • To dispatch official circulars, event invitations, credential badges, and parliamentary updates.
  • To provide responsive communication via WhatsApp and Email for support and inquiries.
  • To detect, prevent, and mitigate spam submissions, malicious bot activity, and unauthorized platform misuse.

04 Data Protection & Security

We adopt rigorous data collection, storage, and processing practices and security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information.

All data exchanges between the browser and our server are encrypted over TLS/HTTPS protocols with upgraded cryptographic ciphers. Access to member registries is restricted strictly to authorized Secretariat officers under non-disclosure obligations.

05 Cookies & Analytical Technologies

Our portal uses essential cookies and aggregated analytics to enhance user experience, remember cookie preferences, and analyze website traffic.

You have full control over cookie permissions via our interactive Cookie Consent banner and can choose to decline non-essential cookies at any time. We do not engage in behavioral cross-site advertising or third-party data tracking.

06 No Sharing or Selling of Personal Data

We do not sell, trade, or rent user personal identification information to any third parties.

We may share generic aggregated demographic information not linked to any personal identification information with educational partners and institutional advisors solely for reporting overall youth participation statistics.

07 Your Rights & Data Access

As an applicant or member, you have the right to request a copy of the information we hold about you, request corrections to erroneous records, or request complete erasure of your data from our active registers by contacting our Secretariat.
