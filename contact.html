Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join as a member?

Click the Become a Member button to complete our official online membership application.

Who is eligible to participate in FLP?

High school, college, and university students across Pakistan aged 14 to 28 who have an interest in leadership, public policy, governance, and civic affairs.

Institutional heads or faculty advisors can contact our Secretariat directly via Email or WhatsApp at +92 328 6444392 for institutional delegation packages.
