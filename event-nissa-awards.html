About the Summit & Nissa Awards

The Women’s Empowerment Summit & Nissa Awards 2026 is Future Leaders Parliament’s flagship initiative dedicated to celebrating female excellence in governance, social innovation, and public service.

Across Pakistan, women are driving transformative reforms in courtrooms, legislative chambers, university research labs, and grassroots communities. The Nissa Awards were established to recognize these extraordinary contributions, offering a prestigious institutional platform that amplifies their voices and inspires millions of aspiring young women leaders.

The upcoming summit will feature high-level panel discussions, keynote addresses by prominent jurists and parliamentarians, masterclasses on leadership and media advocacy, culminating in the grand Nissa Awards presentation ceremony.

🏆 National Juried Selection

Nominations evaluated by an independent jury of senior jurists, academics, and civic leaders.

📜 Verified Citations & Medals

Official FLP parliamentary medal of excellence and commemorative citation presented to all laureates.

🎙️ High-Level Panel Plenaries

Interactive policy sessions debating gender parity in legislation and civic representation.

🤝 Mentorship Cohort 2026

Connecting young women delegates with established female mentors across public institutions.
