Constitutional Governance, Rule of Law & Youth Legal Literacy

An authoritative upcoming masterclass dissecting the constitutional architecture of Pakistan, safeguarding fundamental civil liberties, legislative drafting mechanics, and empowering young scholars to actively uphold the rule of law.

Advocate Nisa Ara is a highly respected legal scholar and litigator with extensive experience in constitutional law, public interest litigation, and parliamentary rights advocacy. Her keynote will provide youth delegates with an indispensable framework for understanding legal remedies, citizen duties, and constitutional accountability.

01. Overview

About The Session

The Department of Law & Parliamentary Affairs has scheduled this dedicated national webinar to bridge the gap between academic legal knowledge and actionable parliamentary literacy for youth leaders across Pakistan.

During this two-hour intensive digital convention, delegates from over 35 universities will engage in live deliberations regarding constitutional safeguards, the hierarchy of courts, procedural lawmaking in the Senate and National Assembly, and how youth can draft actionable policy briefs that align with national statutes.

02. Agenda Breakdown

Session Modules & Discussions

Module 1 • 30 Mins Constitutional Core

Fundamental Rights under the 1973 Constitution (Articles 8–28)

Detailed analysis of fundamental civil liberties including Article 9 (Security of Person), Article 10-A (Right to Fair Trial), Article 19 (Freedom of Speech), and Article 19-A (Right to Information), highlighting judicial enforcement mechanisms under Article 199 and Article 184(3).

Module 2 • 35 Mins Keynote Address

Keynote Address by Advocate Nisa Ara: Bridging Youth & The Legal Realm

Advocate Nisa Ara elaborated on practical case studies of constitutional supremacy, how future policymakers must evaluate proposed bills against constitutional thresholds, and career avenues for young lawyers in legislative counsel.

Module 3 • 30 Mins Interactive Floor

Interactive Delegate Q&A & Hypothetical Legal Scenarios

Open-floor questions from law students regarding judicial review, balancing national security with privacy rights, digital freedom laws, and procedures for drafting public interest litigation petitions.

Module 4 • 25 Mins Resolutions

Adoption of the FLP Youth Legal Literacy Resolution

Formal adoption of a 5-point youth legal manifesto committing FLP shadow cabinet delegates to promote civic constitutional clinics across regional colleges and schools.

03. Key Takeaways

Actionable Takeaways for Delegates

1
Constitutional Literacy

Mastery of citizen rights under Chapter 1 of the Constitution and knowing how to assert them without fear.

2
Legislative Scrutiny

Evaluating private bills for constitutional compliance, proportionality, and non-discrimination.

3
Right to Information

Utilizing Article 19-A RTI requests to demand institutional transparency and public record disclosures.

4
Shadow Cabinet Briefs

Writing formal legal advisories and dissenting opinions on upcoming federal and provincial legislation.
