Institutional Programmes

Flagship Initiatives

From simulation plenaries to regional roundtables and empirical policy authoring, FLP organizes high-impact platforms for emerging youth leaders.

INITIATIVE #01 Simulation

Parliamentary Plenary Assemblies

Multi-day simulation sessions replicating the National and Provincial Assemblies of Pakistan. Delegates serve as Members of Parliament, debate pending bills, and negotiate legislative resolutions under strict parliamentary rules of order.

Quarterly Cycles 300+ Delegates
INITIATIVE #02 Grassroots

Regional Youth Forums & Roundtables

District-level consultations and divisional town halls bringing together student leaders, civic advocates, and local administrators to discuss regional socio-economic challenges.

All Divisions Town Hall Format
INITIATIVE #03 Training

Civic & Diplomatic Boot Camps

Intensive masterclasses led by seasoned legislators, constitutional lawyers, and foreign service diplomats covering bilateral treaty negotiations, speechwriting, and parliamentary rules.

Masterclass Format Certified Badges
INITIATIVE #04 Research

Youth Policy Whitepapers & Audits

Empirical policy dossiers authored by shadow cabinet ministers and research fellows, addressing real-world governance dilemmas submitted directly to parliamentary standing committees.

Annual Volumes Policy Audits
