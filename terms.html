Terms & Conditions

Please review the rules, principles, and parliamentary standards governing your membership, participation in summits, and engagement with Future Leaders Parliament.

Effective & Last Updated: October 2026

01 Acceptance of Agreement

By accessing this platform, submitting a membership application, or attending any summit, webinar, or committee session organized by Future Leaders Parliament (FLP), you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions and our associated Parliamentary Charter.

If you disagree with any portion of these terms, you must refrain from using the platform or participating in FLP-sponsored delegations.

02 Membership & Code of Conduct

Future Leaders Parliament operates on mutual respect, constitutional dignity, non-partisanship, and meritocratic governance. All delegates and executive officers must uphold:

  • Mutual Respect & Inclusivity: Zero tolerance for hate speech, discrimination, sectarianism, harassment, or verbal hostility in all parliamentary discourse.
  • Non-Partisan Objectivity: FLP is an independent youth leadership forum. Members agree not to use the organization’s platform for political partisan propaganda.
  • Integrity & Responsibility: Delegates are expected to fulfill commitments, attend assigned departmental sessions, and represent the organization honorably.
  • Confidentiality: Internal deliberations, policy drafts, and strategic materials must not be leaked or shared without explicit authorization from the Secretariat.
