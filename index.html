Transparency & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Future Leaders Parliament (FLP)? ▼

FLP is an institutional youth forum designed to equip Pakistani youth with statecraft skills, constitutional literacy, parliamentary debating decorum, and public policy formulation expertise.

Is FLP affiliated with any political party? ▼

No. Future Leaders Parliament is strictly independent, non-partisan, and meritocratic. We do not promote or represent any political party. Our focus is national advancement and democratic education.

Who is eligible to apply for membership? ▼

Students, young professionals, lawyers, researchers, and aspiring civic leaders aged 16 to 32 across all provinces of Pakistan and overseas diaspora are eligible to apply.

How do I get involved with the Women’s Empowerment Summit & Nissa Awards 2026? ▼

Visit our dedicated Nissa Awards 2026 page for the latest updates on speaker panels, delegate registration, and nomination guidelines.

How can institutional delegations register? ▼

Colleges, universities, and youth organizations can contact our Secretariat Desk directly via WhatsApp at +92 328 6444392 or email us at futureleadersparliament@gmail.com.
